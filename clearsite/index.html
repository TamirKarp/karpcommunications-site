Safe and straightforward

What I never do.

I never log in to your website, get past password-protected pages or look at pupil data. ClearSite only reads the public website that parents and inspectors can already see.

Your website's content is never used to train AI models.

The report is guidance, checked by a person. It isn't legal advice, and your school remains responsible for what it publishes.

Questions

Common questions.

Which schools is ClearSite for? +

Schools in England: maintained schools and academies, whether primary, secondary or special. The check is tailored to your type of school, so anything that doesn't apply is marked as not applicable.

Do you need access to our website? +

No. ClearSite only reads what's publicly visible, the same as any parent or inspector.

How long does it take? +

Usually within two working hours of confirming your order, during term time. If it's going to take longer, I'll let you know.

What if something in the report looks wrong? +

Every finding links to the page or document it's based on, so you can check it. If you think I've got something wrong, reply to my email and I'll look again and correct the report.

Can we share the report with governors? +

Yes. The report is yours to share with governors, staff and your website provider.

How do we pay? +

I'll email an invoice with your report. Your school can pay by bank transfer or raise a purchase order.
