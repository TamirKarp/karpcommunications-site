FAQ

Common questions.

What if we already have someone who handles social media? +

The Zero-Friction Workflow reduces burden, not creates conflict. In most schools, a member of staff spends 3–5 hours per week on digital tasks outside their role. I take that work entirely. If there is genuine overlap, we scope accordingly — you only pay for what I deliver.

Can we start on Foundation and move up? +

Yes. Most clients start at Digital Partner, but Foundation is a valid entry point. Tier upgrades happen at any point with 30 days' notice. In practice, the Digital Partner scope tends to expand rather than contract once in place.

Do you work with primary schools? +

Yes. All three tiers are available to primary schools. Foundation is typically the right starting point for smaller primaries. Strategic is designed for larger primaries, federations, or those with governing board AI governance obligations.

What about Multi-Academy Trusts? +

MAT engagements are scoped individually under the Strategic tier. A single contract covering 3–6 schools is available at a blended rate. Contact me directly for a bespoke proposal.

Are you available during school hours? +

Yes — this is a core value proposition. As a B2B partner rather than a freelancer working evenings, I am available during business hours for urgent requests, vendor calls, and platform issues.

What compliance credentials do you hold? +

Enhanced DBS, Level 1 Safeguarding, and Prevent Duty training are all current. A GDPR-compliant Data Processing Agreement is signed at onboarding. All work is contracted as B2B consultancy services, outside IR35. Certificates available on request.
